The time angle

Three friends founded Zeitwinkel in Saint-Imier, Bernese Jura. They are still there. Still independent. Still managing the company they started together.

The Workshop

No more than one hundred watches are completed each year. Each one is assembled, regulated and tested by hand. The same watchmaker handles every movement from the first component to the first heartbeat.

In-house manufacture

German Silver

Untreated nickel silver. Harder than brass. Warmer than steel. The plates and bridges of every Zeitwinkel movement require the highest precision in processing and decoration. Our manufacture calibers ZW0102 and ZW0103 are developed, manufactured and assembled in the Swiss Jura.
